# 19: Published Posts

**What to build:** A Lab member records that an approved Rendered Video was posted: platform, account, publication date, URL and affiliate link. Social accounts and affiliate links are kept as reusable records. The member sees every Published Post for a Variant, Product or Campaign.

**Blocked by:** 11, 18

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Social accounts (platform and handle) and affiliate links can be created and reused
- [ ] A Published Post requires an approved Rendered Video, an account, a date and a URL; an affiliate link is optional
- [ ] Recording a Published Post for a Rendered Video that is not approved is refused
- [ ] The same Rendered Video on three accounts is three Published Posts
- [ ] The same URL cannot be recorded twice
- [ ] Published Posts can be listed and filtered by Campaign, Product, Variant, platform and account
- [ ] The form warns when the chosen affiliate link is already used by another Published Post, since Commission can then not be shown per post
- [ ] The publishing-provider interface exists with no implementation; nothing is posted automatically
