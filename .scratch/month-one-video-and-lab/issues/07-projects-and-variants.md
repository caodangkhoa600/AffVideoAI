# 07: Projects and Variants

**What to build:** A member creates a Project from one Product with audience, language, target duration and objective, then adds Variants to it, each with a creative template and a Hook. A Variant can be duplicated with a different Hook.

**Blocked by:** 04

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Project can be created from a Product and lists its Variants
- [ ] Target duration is limited to 15–30 seconds; language is Vietnamese
- [ ] A Variant requires a creative template and a Hook
- [ ] Duplicating a Variant asks for a new Hook and creates a separate Variant
- [ ] A Variant's identifier never changes
- [ ] Projects can be listed, opened and deleted with confirmation
- [ ] A member of another Organization is refused Projects and Variants by identifier
