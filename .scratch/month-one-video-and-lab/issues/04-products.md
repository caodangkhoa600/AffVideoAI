# 04: Products

**What to build:** A member creates, edits, archives, lists, searches and filters Products, with name, category, brand, description, optional price and currency, original URL, optional affiliate URL, target audience and tags.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Product can be created, viewed, edited and archived from the web app
- [ ] The list supports search by name, filter by category and status, and pagination
- [ ] Invalid input returns field-level validation errors shown next to the fields
- [ ] Price is stored as a decimal with an explicit currency
- [ ] Product URLs are stored as text and never fetched
- [ ] A member of another Organization is refused read, edit and archive of the Product by identifier
- [ ] The seed adds the fictional AirBeat X1 Product to the demonstration Organization
