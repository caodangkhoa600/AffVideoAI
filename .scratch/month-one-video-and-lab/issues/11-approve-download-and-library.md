# 11: Approve, download and video library

**What to build:** A member approves a Rendered Video and downloads the MP4. A library lists all Rendered Videos with search, filter, sort, preview, download and delete.

**Blocked by:** 09

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Rendered Video that is ready for review can be approved; approval records who and when and is written to the audit log
- [ ] Download is refused until the Rendered Video is approved; preview works before approval
- [ ] The downloaded file is the same MP4 that was previewed
- [ ] The library can be searched by Product or Project name, filtered by state and creative template, and sorted by date
- [ ] Deleting a Rendered Video asks for confirmation and removes the stored file
- [ ] A member of another Organization is refused approval, download and delete by identifier
