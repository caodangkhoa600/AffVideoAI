# 05: Other takes, and using Stills and Clips again

**What to build:** A member asks for another take of a Clip and chooses which take a Scene uses. A Still or Clip made for a Project is used by every Variant of that Project without being made again, and a member can pick one of the Organization's saved Stills or Clips for a Scene of another Project.

**Blocked by:** 04

**Status:** needs-triage

- [ ] Another take is the member's action, is estimated and confirmed, and counts against the Project's ceiling
- [ ] Choosing a different take creates the next Storyboard version
- [ ] A second Variant of the Project, and a duplicated Variant, use the Project's Stills and Clips and generate nothing
- [ ] A member can list the Organization's saved Stills and Clips and pick one for a Scene of another Project
- [ ] A picked Still or Clip costs nothing and writes no new cost record
- [ ] A Still or Clip that a Storyboard version uses cannot be deleted
