# 04: Generated footage

**What to build:** A second kind of generated Scene: a whole Scene filled with a Clip in which the Product does not appear, such as a setting or a person. Its approved Still becomes a Clip, made by a fake video provider under the same estimate, confirmation and ceiling, and the member watches the Clip before rendering. A Hybrid video has at most two generated Scenes.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** needs-triage

- [ ] Each creative template states which of its Scenes may be generated and with which Technique
- [ ] The planning engine assigns a generated Technique to at most two Scenes, the Hook Scene first
- [ ] A Clip is made only from an approved Still, and is asked for at its Scene's length
- [ ] The member can watch a Clip before rendering
- [ ] Audio that a provider attaches to a Clip is removed
- [ ] Each Clip has a Production cost record
- [ ] A member of another Organization is refused a Clip by identifier, including its file
- [ ] A render of a Storyboard version whose generated-footage Scene has no Clip fails validation with a reason
- [ ] A generated-footage Scene renders the Clip full frame with the Scene's type over it and no Product
- [ ] The template's descriptions ask for invented adults only and for no Product
- [ ] A generated Scene whose description depicts what the Product does must cite a Confirmed Fact; validation rejects it otherwise
- [ ] Withdrawing that Fact marks the Storyboard versions and Rendered Videos that used the Clip as Flagged for Review
- [ ] The three creative templates each have descriptions for their generated Scenes
