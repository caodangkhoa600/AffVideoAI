# 02: Kling's terms on commercial use

**What to do:** Read, in the original wording, what Kling and fal.ai say about using generated video in paid advertisements, and decide whether a Commercial made with Kling may be sold to customers. A summary of Kling's consumer terms (sections 3.1d and 4.6 of `https://kling.ai/docs/user-policy`) says commercial or advertising use needs written permission and that output must be labelled as Kling-generated. fal.ai marks its Kling endpoints "Commercial use" and says the licence is per model (`https://docs.fal.ai/model-apis/faq`, `https://fal.ai/legal/terms-of-service`). Which of the two governs video obtained through fal.ai's API was not established. If the pages do not settle it, ask fal.ai in writing.

This does not block building or the provider test. It blocks opening Commercials to paying Organizations. If Kling cannot be used, the video model changes to Wan 3.0 or Seedance by configuration, and that model's terms are read the same way.
