# Writing is recorded, not confirmed

When a language model writes text (a Project's Storyboard, a Commercial's creative direction), the member is not shown an estimate and is not asked to confirm. The cost is recorded as Production cost and counts against the ceiling. A confirmation is asked only before a picture, a video, speech or music is generated. The rule until now was that a member confirms before anything paid starts; we narrowed it because a writing costs about a cent, and asking for a cent teaches a member to agree without reading, which weakens the confirmation that matters, the one before Clips.

## Consequences

- Writing that fails validation is asked for again by the system, at most twice, with the reason. This is the one paid thing that is retaken without the member. Pictures, video, speech and music are still never retaken automatically (ADR 0003).
- Every writing has a Production cost record, the rejected ones included, so the totals stay honest.
- If the price of writing rises to where a member would want to be asked, this decision is reopened.
