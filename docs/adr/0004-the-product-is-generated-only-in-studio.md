# The Product is generated only in Studio

In Product Lock and in Hybrid the Product is always its photo: generated pictures go behind it or into Scenes it does not appear in. Only Studio, whose video is a Commercial (ADR 0005), may show a generated Product, and there each such Scene carries the Generated Product mark. The founder wants members to be able to make a commercial like the reference, in which a person holds the Product, and that cannot be composited from a photo. We kept the exception out of Hybrid because video generated from a single Product photo distorts labels and shapes, and "the Product is never wrong" is what sets Hybrid apart from other AI video tools.

## Consequences

- Hybrid has no Technique that starts from a Product photo. Image-to-video left the Technique list.
- A Hybrid video cannot show a person holding or using the Product.
- In Studio, a Scene with a generated Product is accepted by a member one at a time, beside the real photo, and is the Organization's responsibility. The system refuses to try unless the Product has enough photos.
- Studio needs reference-guided generation, which Hybrid does not.
- If generated Products prove unreliable even with references, Studio falls back to showing the Product only where its photo can be composited: standing on a surface, pack shots and the end card.
