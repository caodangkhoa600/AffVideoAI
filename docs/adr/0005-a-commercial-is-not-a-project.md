# A Commercial is not a Project

What Studio makes is a Commercial: a thing of its own beside Project, belonging to one Product. It is written fresh by the language model from a brief, with no creative template, no Variant and no Render Mode, and it reuses Scene, Still, Clip, Narration and Production cost. We chose this over a third Render Mode, or a kind of Project, because a Project is defined by rules a Commercial breaks: 15 to 30 seconds in 9:16, a creative template that fixes the Scenes, Variants that differ by their Hook, and at most two generated Scenes. Making Project carry both would have put an exception on each of those rules. A Commercial also has a workflow a Project does not: a creative direction, then Characters and a Setting, then a Still for every Scene, then Clips, each approved before the next is paid for.

## Consequences

- A Commercial is 30 seconds, in 16:9 or 9:16 chosen at creation, and may show a Generated Product (ADR 0004). None of this reaches Project.
- There is no Variant of a Commercial. A second treatment is a second Commercial, and is paid for as one.
- The ceiling on generation belongs to the Commercial, as a Project's belongs to the Project.
- What comes out is a Rendered Video, so approval, the Library, Published Posts and the Affiliate Lab apply unchanged. Where the Lab groups by creative template, Commercials are a group of their own.
- A Still or Clip may now belong to a Commercial as well as to a Project.
- Studio, Basic and Pro stay price-list names. Commercial is the domain term.
